How it works

1

Pin and connect

Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's top bar, pin SaveShot, and connect your Google Drive once.

2

Select area or tab

Drag across part of a page (or press Enter for the full tab) or capture the visible tab in one click.

3

Saved straight to Drive

Screenshots upload directly from Chrome to Chrome_SaveShot_ScreenShots in your Drive and copy the share link.

4

Edit, redact, or download

The editor opens automatically so you can annotate, black out sensitive info, rename, click Download to keep a local PNG, or click Save.

Keyboard shortcuts

Select area Alt + Shift + S
Visible tab Alt + Shift + V

One or more shortcuts are not assigned in Chrome yet (for example if another extension already uses those keys, or after loading unpacked). Click Change shortcuts to set them.

Good to know

Does SaveShot use any external servers or track my data?

No. SaveShot runs 100% locally in Chrome and uploads directly to Google Drive's official API. There are no SaveShot servers, no analytics, and no tracking. With Google's narrow drive.file scope, it cannot see any other files in your Drive.

Can I redact sensitive details before the first upload?

Yes. By default, screenshots upload right away and open in the editor so your link is ready immediately. If you prefer to redact before anything is uploaded, turn on Open editor before uploading in Settings.

Why are buttons disabled on some tabs?

Chrome blocks all extensions from running on internal pages like chrome://settings and on the Chrome Web Store. For local PDFs or file:// pages, turn on Allow access to file URLs in SaveShot's settings at chrome://extensions.

What happens if my Google Drive is full or offline?

If an upload fails after taking a screenshot, SaveShot shows a Save PNG locally button so you can save the image to your computer without losing it.

How does Incognito mode work?

If you turn on Allow in Incognito in chrome://extensions, screenshots still upload directly to your Google Drive while page URLs and local upload history are left out for privacy.

Report a bug or request a feature

Found something broken or want a new option? Open a pre-filled issue on GitHub, or copy the report text if you prefer not to use GitHub.
