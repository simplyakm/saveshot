How it works

1

Pin and connect

Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's top bar, pin SaveShot, and connect your Google Drive.

2

Capture a tab or area

Use the toolbar popup or keyboard shortcuts to capture the full visible tab or drag across part of a page.

3

Saved to Google Drive

Screenshots go into a Chrome_SaveShot_ScreenShots folder in your Drive, and the link copies to your clipboard.

Keyboard shortcuts

Capture visible tab Alt+Shift+S
Select area on page Alt+Shift+R

Good to know

Can SaveShot see my other Google Drive files?

No. SaveShot uses the narrow drive.file permission. It can only access the folder and screenshots that SaveShot creates itself.

Why are buttons disabled on some tabs?

Chrome blocks all extensions from running on internal pages like chrome://settings and on the Chrome Web Store. For local PDFs or file:// pages, turn on Allow access to file URLs in SaveShot's settings at chrome://extensions.

What happens if my Google Drive is full or offline?

If an upload fails after taking a screenshot, SaveShot shows a Save PNG locally button so you can save the image to your computer instead of losing it.

How does Incognito mode work?

If you turn on Allow in Incognito in chrome://extensions, screenshots still upload to your Google Drive, while page URLs and local upload history are left out for privacy.
