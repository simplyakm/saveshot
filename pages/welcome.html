How it works

1

Pin and connect

Click the puzzle icon in Chrome's top bar, pin SaveShot, and connect your Google Drive once.

2

Select area or tab

Drag across part of a page or capture the full visible tab from the popup or keyboard shortcuts.

3

Saved to Google Drive

Screenshots go into Chrome_SaveShot_ScreenShots in your Drive and copy the link automatically.

Keyboard shortcuts

Select area Alt + Shift + R
Visible tab Alt + Shift + S

Good to know

Can I annotate, redact, rotate, or rename screenshots?

Yes. Click Edit after taking a screenshot (or on any item in Recent) to add arrows, shapes, text, black-bar redactions, custom hex colors, rotate or flip, rename the file, and update the file in Google Drive while keeping the same link. Turn on Open editor before uploading in Settings to redact before initial upload.

Can SaveShot see my other Google Drive files?

No. SaveShot uses the narrow drive.file permission. It can only access the folder and screenshots that SaveShot creates itself.

Why are buttons disabled on some tabs?

Chrome blocks all extensions from running on internal pages like chrome://settings and on the Chrome Web Store. For local PDFs or file:// pages, turn on Allow access to file URLs in SaveShot's settings at chrome://extensions.

What happens if my Google Drive is full or offline?

If an upload fails after taking a screenshot, SaveShot shows a Save PNG locally button so you can save the image to your computer without losing it.

How does Incognito mode work?

If you turn on Allow in Incognito in chrome://extensions, screenshots still upload to your Google Drive while page URLs and local upload history are left out for privacy.
