SaveShot

Private screenshots straight from Chrome to your Google Drive.

SaveShot captures any selected screen area or visible browser tab, opens a fast built-in annotation and redaction editor, and uploads PNGs directly from Chrome to your own Google Drive with zero external servers and zero tracking.

Add to Chrome Privacy Policy GitHub Source

Select Area or Full Tab

Drag across any region of a webpage (Alt+Shift+S) or capture the visible tab in one click (Alt+Shift+V), with pixel-exact HiDPI and zoom accuracy.

Annotate, Redact & Rename

Add arrows, boxes, circles, lines, freehand strokes, text, custom hex colors, or solid black pixel redactions, and save straight back to the same Google Drive link.

100% Local in Chrome

Screenshots go directly from your browser to Google's official Drive API (https://www.googleapis.com). SaveShot has no private servers, analytics, or ads.

Narrow drive.file Scope

SaveShot only accesses the screenshots and folder (Chrome_SaveShot_ScreenShots) it creates itself. It cannot view or modify any other files in your Google Drive.
